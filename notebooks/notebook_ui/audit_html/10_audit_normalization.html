STEP 10

Initial Text Normalization

Creating a normalized version only for detecting hidden duplicates.

The back side explains normalization operations and storage location.

What Changes Does This Function Apply?

ی

Unifies different forms of the Persian character "ی".

ک

Unifies Arabic and Persian forms of "ک".

◌

Removes invisible characters.

‌

Converts zero-width characters into spaces for comparison.

␠

Converts repeated spaces into a single space.

No punctuation marks, numbers, or words are removed.

df_raw

Remains unchanged.

audit_df

Stores the analyzed version.
