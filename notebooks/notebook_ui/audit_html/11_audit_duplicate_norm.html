STEP 11

Normalized Duplicate Text Analysis

Identifying texts that become identical after character and spacing normalization.

The back side explains the difference between exact duplicates and hidden duplicates.

Difference From Exact Duplicates

Two sentences may appear different to a computer even though they look identical to humans.

این محصول کیفیت خوبی دارد
اين محصول كيفيت خوبي دارد

After unifying Arabic and Persian characters, these two sentences become the same text. These cases are called hidden duplicates.
