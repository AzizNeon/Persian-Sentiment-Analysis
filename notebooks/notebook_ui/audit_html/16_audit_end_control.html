STEP 16

Final Raw Data Integrity Check

Verifying that the original dataset remains unchanged during the audit process.

The back side explains the role of this check in protecting raw data.

What Does This Check Confirm?

audit_df

All normalization steps, calculations, and helper columns are applied only to this version.

df_raw

The original dataset remains unchanged without deletion, correction, or content modification.

Matching row counts and the final fingerprint with the initial values confirm that the raw dataset has not been modified.
