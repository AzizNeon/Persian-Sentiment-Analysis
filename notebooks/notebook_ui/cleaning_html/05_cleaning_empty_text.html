STEP 05

Remove Empty Text Samples

Rows whose text becomes empty after normalization are identified and removed from the cleaned dataset.

The back side explains the reason for removal and deleted row storage.

Why Are These Rows Removed?

No Meaningful Content

Rows that contain no text after removing spaces and invisible characters cannot provide useful information for model training.

Raw Data Protection

Removal is performed only from df_clean and df_raw remains unchanged.

Removed rows are stored in removed_empty_text_rows so the cleaning process remains traceable and reportable.
