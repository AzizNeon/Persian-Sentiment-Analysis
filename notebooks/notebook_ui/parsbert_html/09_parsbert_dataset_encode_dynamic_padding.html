Why Dynamic Padding?

Most SentiPers texts are much shorter than the maximum length of 128 tokens. Therefore, fixed padding to the maximum length creates unnecessary computation. Dynamic Padding extends each batch only to the length of its longest sample.

Based on the previous analysis, MAX_LENGTH = 128 was selected with coverage of 99.5% of Train samples.
