STEP 07

Load ParsBERT Tokenizer

The official ParsBERT tokenizer is loaded to convert Persian texts into model-compatible tokens.

Hover over the card to view the details.

What Does the Tokenizer Do?

ParsBERT does not receive raw Persian text directly. The tokenizer first splits text into smaller WordPiece tokens, and each token is mapped to a numerical identifier from the model vocabulary.

At this stage, only tokenizer behavior is analyzed. Final truncation and padding are not applied yet.
