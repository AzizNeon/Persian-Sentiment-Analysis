STEP 11

Short Training Process Test

A few training steps are executed on CPU before full Fine-tuning to verify Backpropagation and Optimizer behavior.

Hover over the card to view the details.

Why Perform a Smoke Test?

ParsBERT contains more than 162 million parameters, and full training on a local system can be expensive. Therefore, a small batch is first used to confirm that Loss calculation, Gradient computation, and weight updates work correctly.

Results from this stage are experimental and are not considered part of the final model performance.
